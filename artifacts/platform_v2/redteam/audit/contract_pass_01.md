## Note on CRIT-9 packet exposure

`artifacts/platform_v2/redteam/audit_packet_contract.json` (the packet supplied for this audit)
was generated from a study compiled before or without the packet regeneration that adds
`outcome.semantics.resolution_precedence` — its `outcome` block (lines 170-210) carries
`horizon_end_rule`, `resolution_precedence` is **absent** at the top level but present under
`semantics.resolution_precedence` is also absent from the supplied packet; the packet's
`outcome.semantics` key does not appear at all. Cross-checking against
`research_workflow/audit_packets_v2.py:139-140` (this branch's `contract_packet()`) confirms the
code *would* emit `outcome.semantics.resolution_precedence` for a freshly-compiled plan — the
supplied packet is simply stale relative to head `77e94d20`. This does not indicate a code defect;
it means the audit packet the orchestrator handed to this pass predates the fix. Flagging as
`NOT VERIFIED` on the packet itself, `CLOSED` on the code+test evidence.
