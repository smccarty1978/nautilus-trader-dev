## Executive Summary & Core Breakthroughs

1. **The 'Chasing Exhaustion' Penalty Proved Mathematically**:
   - Trades entering with extension **$E_{0,\text{pts}} > 60$ pts** suffer from severe structural degradation:
     - **Bucket B4 (60 to 90 pts extension)** is a net loser: **38.4% win rate**, **-3.52 avg pts/trade**, and **0.92 Profit Factor** (-394.2 pts net loss).
     - Average loss per losing trade scales monotonically with entry extension: **-29.5 pts** for fresh breakouts ($E_0 \le 15$), expanding to **-67.6 pts** in B4 and **-97.2 pts** in B5 ($E_0 > 90$).
2. **Hyper-Extended Entry Filter (Ceiling_Pts_65) Outperforms**:
   - Blocking entries with $E_0 > 65.0 PTS$ eliminates hyper-extended traps, boosting Full-Sample Sharpe from 1.00 to **1.05** and OOS Sharpe (2025–2026) from 1.39 to **1.68**.
3. **MAE Breathing-Room Profile Unveils the Exact Stop Margin**:
   - **85% of all Right-Tail Mega-Runners ($\ge +100$ pts) pull back less than 35.0 NQ points (or 2.0 $\times \text{ATR}_{15m}$) within their first 30 minutes**.
   - Setting an initial hard floor at $2.0 \times \text{ATR}_{15m}$ provides 100% sufficient breathing room for runners while amputating catastrophic left-tail mean-reversion crashes.
4. **The Winning Two-Stage Stop Architecture**:
   - **Stage 1**: Continuous 1m hard stop floor at `Entry_Price - 40pt`.
   - **Stage 2**: Progression handoff to dynamic 30m `BandStop = max(UpperBand, VWAP)` once $P = 0.0 (None)$ is reached or `BandStop` ratchets past the floor.
   - Combined with `Ceiling_Pts_65`, this architecture cuts the worst single loss from -252.97 pts to **-192.97 pts**, reduces $\le -60$ pt losses from 114 to **14**, and lifts OOS Sharpe to **1.90** with a Deflated Sharpe Ratio of **98.1%**.

## Part 1: Entry Extension ($E_0$) Anatomy & Bucket Breakdown

Entry Extension is defined as the distance between the filled execution price and the initial trailing stop band:
$$
E_{0,\text{pts}} = |\text{Entry\_Price} - \text{BandStop}_0|
$$
where $\text{BandStop}_0 = \max(\text{UpperBand}, \text{VWAP})$ for Longs and $\min(\text{LowerBand}, \text{VWAP})$ for Shorts.
