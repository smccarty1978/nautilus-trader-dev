## Forensics Findings & Adjudication

### 1. The Fork in the Road: Prediction Failure vs. Policy Failure

To resolve whether the atlas is structurally flawed (Prediction Failure) or the prototype policy is failing to monetize valid moves (Policy Failure), we audited the **20 highest-scoring false positives** (losing trades that had high scores) in the OOS (2025–2026) period.

The critical distinction is:
*   **Prediction Failure:** High score but low remaining regime MFE (the atlas's prediction of trend health was wrong; the regime had no opportunity left).
*   **Policy Failure:** High score and high remaining regime MFE (the atlas was correct that the trend was healthy, but the policy failed to monetize the opportunity).

### 3. Recommendations for the Labeling Scheme & Next Steps

1.  **Rename the Classification:** Symmetrically relabel the classes in the diagnostics framework:
    *   **Prediction Failure:** High score AND Low remaining regime MFE (`Remaining MFE < 1.0 ATR`). These are the only trades that genuinely disprove the atlas.
    *   **Policy Failure:** High score AND High remaining regime MFE (`Remaining MFE >= 1.0 ATR`) but losing trade outcome. These represent monetization failures.
2.  **Introduce Defensive Stops & Profit Targets:** The policy must move away from a pure regime-flip exit and incorporate:
    *   **A Trailing Stop or Scale-Out Rule:** To lock in profits when the trade reaches > 1.5 ATR in profit.
    *   **A Hard Stop-Loss:** To protect against the small subset of genuine prediction failures (like 202511533).
