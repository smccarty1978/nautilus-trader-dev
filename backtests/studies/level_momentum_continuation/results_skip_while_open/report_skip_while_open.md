# Single-Position Audit (Skip While In Position)

## Method

Re-aggregates the BE=2.5 + original-stops simulation with a single-position rule: any trigger that fires while a prior trade is still open is SKIPPED.

Comparison: 'Unfiltered' = original simulation that treats every trigger as an independent trade (no position management). 'Skip-while-open' = the realistic single-position view.

Years tested: 2024 (OOS) and 2025 (IS). BE threshold = 2.5 pt. Commission = 0.25 pt. Multiplier = $20.0/pt.
