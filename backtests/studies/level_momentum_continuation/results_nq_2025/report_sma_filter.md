# SMA(20) Trend-Alignment Filter — Level Momentum Study

Source trades: `studies\level_momentum_continuation\results_nq_2025\trades_unfiltered.csv` | n=58,826 (after dropping 5 for SMA warmup)

## Method

- Compute SMA(20) on 1m closes (causal: SMA at bar i uses closes [i-19 .. i] inclusive).
- For each trigger bar, check the close vs the SMA value at that same bar.
- **Aligned** = (long AND close > SMA) OR (short AND close < SMA).
- **Unaligned** = the opposite.
- Hypothesis: aligned trades should have higher WR, tighter winner MAE, better mean PnL.
- Trades within first 19 bars of data dropped (SMA warmup).
