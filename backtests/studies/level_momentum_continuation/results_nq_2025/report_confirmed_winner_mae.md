## Subset definition

- `first_bar_winner == 1` (first bar closed favorably for trade direction)
- `outcome == "win"` (target hit before stop within 120 bars)

MAE = max adverse excursion across the FULL trade lifetime (entry to target hit), in absolute points.

**p99 MAE** = the worst drawdown that 99% of these trades stayed within. A stop wider than this would preserve >=99% of these wins; tighter would clip some.
