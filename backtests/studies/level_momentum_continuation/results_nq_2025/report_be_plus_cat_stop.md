# Catastrophic SL × BE-Stop Grid — Level Momentum

## Method

Replaces the original 'one prior in sequence' stop with a fixed catastrophic SL (Y pts), combined with a BE-stop activation after MFE >= X pts.

Sweep grid: cat_stop_pts in [5.0, 7.5, 10.0, 12.5, 15.0, 20.0, 25.0, 30.0], be_threshold in [2.5, 5.0, 7.5, 10.0, 0.0] (40 combinations per cell).

BE threshold = 0.0 means NO BE rule (cat stop only).

Per-bar order: BE-armed exit -> cat stop -> target. BE armed at end of bar K, checked from bar K+1.

Commission: 0.25 pts/trade. NQ multiplier: $20.0/pt.
