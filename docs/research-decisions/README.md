# Research Decisions & Permanent Design Records

This directory contains permanent records of quantitative research decisions, structural invariants, and major architectural shifts in the repository.

## What Belongs Here
- Permanent timestamp decisions and timezone rules.
- Population and universe definitions.
- Execution and fill-semantics decisions.
- Feature provenance and snapshot immutability policies.
- Session definitions and RTH/ETH classifications.
- Censoring-policy and same-bar race policy decisions.
- Replay and collector/runtime parity designs.
- Audits and structural refactoring decision notes.

**Crucial:** All records in this directory must be committed to version control and kept up-to-date as the codebase evolves to ensure auditability and reproducibility.
