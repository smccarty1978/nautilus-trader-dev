## Verdict

**Years with at least one slice at PF >= 1.10**: 0 / 3.

**Insufficient cross-year stability** — fewer than 2 years yielded any slice at PF >= 1.10 + n >= 200. Per the success criteria, the fast-resolution edge does not exist in this event family.

### Why it fails (structural)

The race economics require crossing a high PT% threshold for any 2:1 or larger reward/risk to break even. Actual PT% on the 1.0 PT / 0.5 SL race within 120s across all years and entry candidates:

| Slice | PT% | Required for breakeven |
|---|--:|--:|
| 2024 (avg) | ~23% | ~32% |
| 2025 (avg) | ~23% | ~32% |
| 2026 (avg) | ~21% | ~32% |

PT/SL race rate stays at 0.42 (PT/(PT+SL)) across years — the 1.0 ATR PT target within a short window is structurally rare relative to the 0.5 ATR SL hit. The reward/risk asymmetry doesn't compensate.

### HMM finding (descriptive only)

HMM provides modest separation but never enough to clear the threshold:

- **State 2 / low-progress** subset is closest to breakeven (2024 -$17, 2025 -$0.46, 2026 +$4.02 — but n=240/248/60 too small for the spec).
- **Transition INTO state 3** is mixed (2024 -$2.62, 2025 +$10.26 PF 1.15, 2026 -$26.41) — single-year hit, doesn't generalize.
- **Transition OUT OF state 3** is consistently negative (-$15 to -$21/trade across all 3 years). This is a stable NEGATIVE signal but doesn't help us identify positive trades.
- **State 3 in 2026** is the worst single bucket (-$43.36/trade on n=934), consistent with state 3 = vol burst flagged in earlier HMM work.

The HMM state-3 "inversion" finding from the prior pullback study (May reports) does NOT carry over to the fast-resolution race population.

### ML test SKIPPED

Per success criteria ("Continue only if..." failed), the lightweight ML test is not run. Without any descriptive slice meeting the bar, an ML model would just be hill-climbing on noise.

### Branch decision

This event family (HH/LL-confirmed 1m regimes, fast-resolution race exits) is **DEAD**. No path forward without a structural change to the entry signal. The HH/LL-confirmed regime cohort itself appears too low-quality for any short-horizon edge — PT-before-SL within 120s tops out around 23% PT rate, which can't support any practical bracket geometry tested.

Reasons to abandon rather than tune:
1. The PT/SL race rate is stable at ~42% across 3 independent years — not noise variability.
2. Wider stops (1.0/0.75) reduce SL rate but also reduce PT rate at the same window — the absolute spread in resolution timing is the problem, not threshold choice.
3. Earlier entry timing (+0s vs +90s) doesn't help — race outcome is dominated by post-entry path, not entry timing.
4. HMM state separation is real but too small (4-5pp PT%) to clear the 9pp gap to breakeven.
