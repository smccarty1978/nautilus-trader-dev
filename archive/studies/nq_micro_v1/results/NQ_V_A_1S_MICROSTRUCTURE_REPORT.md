## 4. Final verdict

### Direct answers to study questions

**Do winners have higher micro trend efficiency before entry?**
Yes, but with caveats. In the global "winners vs losers" comparison the directional-efficiency / net-move features show small positive Cohen's d (~0.07-0.09 — the volume features dominate). When isolated as a filter (`flip2conf_dir_efficiency >= 0.30`), it's the single strongest signal found in this study. Within-pocket (24-25 W vs 24-25 L), the effect collapses to ~0.02 — the filter discriminates *across pockets* (it lifts losing years to break-even or better) rather than *within them*.

**Do losers have higher sign-flip/chop before entry?**
**No — the opposite.** A vs C reveals that 24-25 winners have *higher* `sign_flip_rate` (0.41 vs 0.39, d +0.25) and *higher* `pct_adverse` (d +0.25). This is a regime/ATR effect: 24-25 had higher 1s-bar volatility, which mechanically produces more sign flips. Sign-flip filters (`w60s_sign_flip_rate <= 0.30`) actively HURT 2024 (+$0.84 vs +$6.35) and 2025 (-$45.97 vs +$18.04). **The chop-suppression hypothesis is falsified.**

**Does volume confirm or diverge?**
Volume confirms, weakly. The top global discriminators are all volume features: `fav_volume`, `total_volume`, `volume_per_s` (d +0.08-0.09). Winners trade in slightly more active 1s windows. But the effect is small and `dir_vol_imbalance > 0` as a filter only retains ~80% of trades and barely moves PnL. Higher volume is a necessary condition but not sufficient.

**Are 2024-2025 winners structurally different from earlier-year/2026 losers?**
**The 2024-2025 vs 2020-2023 difference is real but modest** (top discriminator d ~0.27 — small effect). **The 2020-2023 vs 2026 difference is huge** (top discriminator `conf2fill_price_per_unit_vol`, d -0.83). 2026 is structurally different from all prior years in price-per-volume — i.e., it has much higher volatility per unit of order flow. This matches the prior NQ V_A Viability Classifier finding that 2026 had ATR ~2x the level of 2020-2023.

### Main question — do 1s microstructure features add predictive value beyond registry features?

**Two answers, depending on the model:**

**As ML features: NO.** Walk-forward LGBM with 194 micro features performs *worse* than the same model with 32 registry features:

| Set | Avg AUC | Avg top-10% mean | Top-10% positive folds |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| A (registry, 32 feats) | 0.539 | $41.33 | 3/4 |
| B (micro, 194 feats) | 0.523 | $19.60 | 3/4 |
| C (combined, 226 feats) | 0.534 | $19.09 | 2/4 |

ΔAUC (C-A) = -0.005. Δtop-10% mean = -$22.25. Adding 162 micro features dilutes the signal that a small registry model already captures, classic overfitting in a 226-dim space with ~12K training samples per fold.

**As single-threshold filters: YES.** `flip2conf_dir_efficiency >= 0.30` is the **strongest pre-entry filter found in any V_A study to date:**

| Year | Baseline mean $ | Filtered mean $ | Δ | Filtered total $ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 2020 | -$9.57 | **+$42.21** | +$51.78 | +$16,545 |
| 2021 | -$22.02 | **+$16.36** | +$38.38 | +$7,085 |
| 2022 | -$15.82 | -$39.53 | -$23.72 | -$11,425 |
| 2023 | -$13.28 | **+$13.84** | +$27.12 | +$4,000 |
| 2024 | +$6.35 | **+$11.32** | +$4.98 | +$3,250 |
| 2025 | +$18.04 | **+$93.80** | +$75.76 | +$30,860 |
| 2026 | -$17.23 | **+$45.65** | +$62.88 | +$5,660 |
| **7yr total** | **-$149,280** | **+$55,975** | | |

- **6/7 years positive** (only 2022 fails — high ATR year, signal saturated)
- **2026 OOS: +$45.65/trade** — the first NQ V_A filter to be cleanly positive on the held-out year
- ~10% retention (~300-450 trades/year)
- Best filter at 9.9-12.6% kept, mean +$25/trade across 7 years
- The closest companion `w60s_dir_efficiency >= 0.30` is also strong (5/7 positive, +$31K total) but with one larger negative year (2022 -$58/trade)

### Why filter beats ML

The signal `flip2conf_dir_efficiency >= 0.30` is concentrated in a single causal moment: the 60s between regime flip and confirmation must show clean directional move (not chop). LGBM with 194 features doesn't preferentially weight this — it spreads importance across many noisy correlates. The single threshold is more robust because it captures the structural meaning: "the move from flip to confirmation actually went the right way without retracing."

This is the inverse of what most prior studies showed. Previous filter sweeps (NQ V_A Viability v1, MTF alignment) all collapsed when restricted to 2026 — this one **survives 2026**.

### Recommendation

**This finding warrants NT runtime validation.** Build a NT strategy that requires `flip2conf_dir_efficiency >= 0.30` at the bar+1 confirmation moment in addition to the V_A confirmation rules. Cost model: $5 commission + 1-tick slip. Verify:

1. Same 6/7 positive years on NT runtime
2. 2026 stays positive
3. No micro-feature lookahead snuck in (the parity gate from MEMORY.md must pass)
4. 2022 negative is acceptable / not catastrophic given 6 positive years

**If NT runtime confirms, this is the first deployable V_A variant identified in the entire research series.** Caution: 2022's -$11K (the only loser year) is offset by 2025's +$31K and 2026's +$6K, giving aggregate +$56K. But 2022 is a legitimate concern — the high-ATR regime broke the signal there. A second filter to identify high-ATR exclusion conditions could help, but adding filters risks the "good filter on average, kills 2025" trap from MEMORY.md.

**Do NOT deploy V_A baseline as-is.** This study confirms (yet again) that the unfiltered V_A is structurally negative.

**Do NOT chase the ML angle.** The ML overfitting is consistent with prior studies — 12K-30K training trades cannot support 200+ feature models when the signal is weak.
